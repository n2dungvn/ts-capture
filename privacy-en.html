Privacy policy

Effective: 2026-10-20 · App version: 3.0.4 · Publisher: Nguyễn Ngọc Dũng

TS Capture processes screenshots, annotations and recordings on your device. The app has no feature that uploads this content to a publisher server.

1. Information the app accesses

When you start a capture or recording, TS Capture accesses the region, display or window you select. This can include personal or confidential information visible on screen. If you select an audio mode, the app records Windows audio, a microphone, or both from the selected devices. A new profile defaults to No audio; a saved choice can be reused in a later session.

The app reads audio device names/identifiers and stores your choices, including language, hotkeys, output folders and drawing preferences. No TS Capture account is required. The app includes no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.

2. Why information is processed

Information is used for the screenshot, stitching, editing, playback, audio capture, recording and video optimization actions you request. Hotkeys control these functions; the app does not log typed text. You control the optional launch-at-sign-in setting.

3. Storage and retention

  • Images and annotations in an open session are processed in memory. Saving writes an image to the location you choose or a default folder you explicitly enable.
  • Settings are stored in the TSCapture folder of your Windows LocalAppData user profile. Windows may redirect an MSIX edition to package-specific app storage.
  • Temporary videos are stored in the Recordings subfolder of the app's data profile. Session temporary files are cleaned when you close after saving or confirm discarding. A crash or abnormal shutdown can leave files available for recovery.
  • You manage exported images/videos. The app does not automatically delete those files when uninstalled. The app does not separately encrypt settings or temporary files; access depends on Windows accounts, file permissions and device protection.

4. Clipboard and other applications

Copy image writes an image to the Windows clipboard. Other applications with clipboard access may read it. If you enable Windows clipboard history or synchronization, Windows may retain or sync the content according to your settings. Opening files, folders or links uses Windows default applications, which have their own policies.

5. Sharing and network access

The publisher does not receive or sell images, videos or audio through TS Capture's functions. Bundled FFmpeg/FFprobe process local files; the TS Capture interface has no upload feature. Opening support or software-source links can make your browser access the selected site. The Microsoft Store edition uses Store services to check licensing and trial expiration and to open the app purchase interface. Microsoft handles the Store account and payment; TS Capture receives entitlement status and trial expiration, not payment card details. Images, video and audio are not sent for license checks. Microsoft Store and Windows may process licensing, purchase, installation, update or diagnostic information under Microsoft's policies.

6. Your choices and deletion

You choose the capture/audio source, start or stop recording, mute the microphone and choose where to save. You can revoke microphone access in Windows Settings. Close a session to discard unsaved data and use File Explorer to delete exported files. Open temporary recordings for recovery provides access to temporary videos, which you can delete when no longer needed. To reset settings, exit the app and delete settings.json from the TSCapture profile. Clear the Windows clipboard and clipboard history separately if needed.

7. Children and policy changes

The app does not ask for age information and has no child-specific account or data collection service. Changes to data processing will be reflected in this policy with an updated effective date.

8. Contact
